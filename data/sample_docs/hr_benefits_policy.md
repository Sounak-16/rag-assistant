# Acme Corp Global Employee Benefits & HR Policy

## Health and Dental Coverage
Acme Corp provides two comprehensive healthcare options for full-time employees:
1. Gold Premier Plan: $120 monthly employee premium contribution. Comprehensive coverage with a $500 annual individual deductible, 90% in-network coinsurance, and full prescription drug coverage.
2. Silver Core Plan: $60 monthly employee premium contribution. $1,500 annual individual deductible, 80% in-network coinsurance.
Dental insurance is 100% employer-paid through Delta Dental, providing up to $2,000 per year in preventative and restorative dental treatments.

## Retirement Savings Plan: 401(k)
Employees are eligible to participate in the Acme 401(k) retirement plan through Fidelity immediately upon hire.
Acme matches 100% of employee contributions up to the first 3% of base salary, and 50% on the next 2% of base salary (maximum company match of 4% total).
Employer matching contributions vest immediately on a 100% schedule from day one.

## Paid Time Off (PTO) and Leave Policy
- Annual Paid Time Off: Full-time employees accrue 20 paid vacation days per calendar year, accrued at 1.67 days per month. Up to 5 unused PTO days may roll over into the subsequent calendar year; any excess days beyond 5 expire on December 31.
- Sick Leave: Employees receive 10 dedicated paid sick days annually, available on January 1.
- Paid Parental Leave: Birth, adoptive, and foster parents receive 16 consecutive weeks of fully paid parental leave. Parental leave must be taken within 12 months following the child's birth or legal placement.

## Remote Work and Home Office Stipend
All full-time remote and hybrid employees receive a one-time remote home office setup stipend of $750 upon joining.
This stipend reimburses ergonomic chairs, desks, monitors, and workspace equipment.
In addition, remote employees receive a recurring $75 monthly internet and mobile connectivity reimbursement submitted through Expensify.
